📚 Financial Audit & Methodology Guide

Core Model Assumptions & Calculations

Complete transparent audit of all 5 pillars used to compute Real In-Hand cash, 20-Year Wealth compounding, and Lifetime Human Capital Net Present Value (NPV).

⏱️ 1. Present Value & Inflation Engine

All future salaries and wealth can be discounted back to Day 1 (Age 21) using:
PV = FV / (1 + r)^t

  • Default 6.0% Rate: Represents long-term urban Indian CPI inflation (Real Purchasing Power).
  • 8.5% Hurdle Rate: Matches the borrowing cost of Tier-1 education loans.
  • 0.0% Mode: Reverts to nominal, unadjusted face-value cash flows.
🎯 2. Starting Compensation Baselines

Starting packages anchor the entire 20-year trajectory, using audited campus medians and verified benchmark offers:

  • MBA Colleges (Median CTC): Anchored strictly to the official audited median CTC from campus placement reports (e.g., ₹34.5L for IIM-A, ₹34.0L for IIM-B/C & ISB, ₹32.0L for FMS, ₹26.0L for MDI/IIFT, ₹19.0L for New IIMs, ₹15.5L for Baby IIMs).
  • Pure Undergrad Tracks: Benchmarked across both Pure UG Good Placement (calibrated to the verified ₹14.40L CTC tier: ₹9.0L fixed + ₹90k bonus + ₹4.5L benefits) and official SRCC Median Placement (~₹8.5L CTC), alongside a standard ₹6.0L Big 4 baseline (Venky/Deloitte).
  • Progression Engine (Path B): Uses single-firm organic progression (7–10% annual merit raises + standard 15–20% promotion bumps), without assuming speculative lateral job-switches.
🏛️ 3. Tax Regime (Budget 2025-26)

Full compliance with Section 115BAC (New Tax Regime) enacted across all 20 years:

  • Standard Deduction: ₹75,000 for salaried employees.
  • Section 87A Rebate: 100% tax rebate for taxable income ≤ ₹12.0L. Salaried gross cash up to ₹12.75L is 100% tax-free (Oxane pays ₹0 tax in Year 1 & Year 2!).
  • Slabs: 0-4L (0%), 4-8L (5%), 8-12L (10%), 12-16L (15%), 16-20L (20%), 20-24L (25%), >24L (30%) + 4% cess.
💳 4. MBA Debt & Opportunity Cost

Every MBA program incurs 2 years of zero cash earnings while studying:

  • Loan Tenure: 7 years (84 monthly EMIs) starting Year 3.
  • Interest Rates: 8.5% (IIM A/B/C, ISB, XLRI) and 8.75% (New & Baby IIMs).
  • Monthly EMIs: ₹47,509/mo for IIM-A (₹30L loan); ₹25,540/mo for IIM Amritsar; ₹0 for FMS & SRCC.
📈 5. Portfolio Compounding (@ 9.0%)

Surplus cash after taxes, rent, living expenses, and loan EMIs is invested annually:

  • Progressive Savings Rate: 35% of in-hand cash saved when CTC < ₹30L; 45% when ₹30L–₹75L; 60% when > ₹75L.
  • Net Portfolio Return: 9.0% post-tax blended CAGR (65% Equity Index Funds @ 11.5%, 25% Debt/EPF @ 7.5%, 10% Gold/Liquid).
🚀 6. Multi-Phase Growth Curve (S-Curve)

Salaries follow an empirical 3-phase corporate S-curve calibrated across 20-year alumni trajectories:

  • Phase 1 (Y1–5: Early Acceleration): High initial ramp. Tier-1 MBAs grow at g1 ≈ 17.5%–18.2%; Pure UG advances via Analyst → Sr. Analyst (+20% at M24).
  • Phase 2 (Y6–10: Mid-Career Plateau): Progression to Manager / AVP. Moderates to g2 ≈ 12.5%–13.2% for Top MBAs vs. 10.8%–11.8% for UG (+18% Manager jump at M96).
  • Phase 3 (Y11–20: Divergence & Glass Ceiling): Top MBAs compound at g3 ≈ 8.0%–8.2% scaling to Partner / MD / CXO (₹1.8Cr–₹2.5Cr+ CTC). Undergrads face a natural ceiling at Director / Principal (~₹75L–₹95L CTC, g3 ≈ 5.5%–6.8%).
